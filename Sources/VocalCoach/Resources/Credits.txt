Vocal Coach credits

Acoustic analysis
Vocal Coach adapts the Burg and polynomial-root steps of Phonia's formant
analysis to Swift under the MIT license. See Phonia-MIT.txt.
https://github.com/mkpoli/phonia/tree/663f43de63dd9dcb82a124b34739edb52635ebb6/crates/phonia-formant

Voice synthesis
Vocal Coach uses PageHum’s Apple Silicon CLI for Kokoro 82M with Emma (bf_emma).
The included PageHumVoice directory contains its license and dependency notices.
https://github.com/Mieraidihaimu/pagehum
https://huggingface.co/hexgrad/Kokoro-82M/blob/main/VOICES.md

Recorded examples
All clips were converted to mono PCM WAV and adjusted for playback volume.
Each adapted clip is available under the same license as its source.
